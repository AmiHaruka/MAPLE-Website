NequIP

NequIP uses E(3)-equivariant tensor convolutions to carry directional information between atomic environments. In the original study, these tensor channels improved accuracy and data efficiency compared with scalar-only control networks.

NequIP Pretrained Models

The three NequIP models available in MAPLE were trained on PeptideCS2 energy and force data. Their names identify the peptide training subset and the gas-phase or COSMO-water reference environment.

Keyword Training / Reference Chemical Coverage
nequip-t3-cosmo PeptideCS2 tripeptides; BP86/dgauss-dzvp + D3(BJ), COSMO water (ε = 80) H, C, N, O, S
nequip-t123-cosmo PeptideCS2 mono-, di- and tripeptides; BP86/dgauss-dzvp + D3(BJ), COSMO water (ε = 80) H, C, N, O, S
nequip-t3-gas PeptideCS2 tripeptides; BP86/dgauss-dzvp + D3(BJ), gas phase H, C, N, O, S

Allegro and Allegro-FM

Allegro builds many-body correlations through successive equivariant tensor products within a fixed atomic neighbourhood. Increasing network depth leaves the spatial cutoff unchanged, allowing independent evaluation of neighbourhoods and efficient spatial parallelisation.

Allegro-FM is a pretrained model series built with Allegro. The two available models combine MPtrj inorganic data at the PBE/plane-wave level and OFF23 molecular data at ωB97M-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVPPD.

Keyword Training / Reference Chemical Coverage
allegro-fm-256 Inorganic crystals at PBE; organic molecules at ωB97M-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVPPD 89 elements: Z = 1–83 and 89–94
allegro-fm-512 Inorganic crystals at PBE; organic molecules at ωB97M-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVPPD 89 elements: Z = 1–83 and 89–94

Choosing the Reference Environment

The COSMO-water models learn the solvent contribution already included in the PeptideCS2 reference calculations.

All five models require charge 0 and multiplicity 1.

Calculator Capabilities

  • Hessian and HVP: the three NequIP and two Allegro-FM models provide analytic Hessians by default, numerical Hessians, and Hessian–vector products.
  • Periodic systems: NequIP and Allegro-FM support periodic cells, with stress and virial output.

Usage

#model=allegro-fm-256
#sp

XYZ structure.xyz
#model=nequip-t3-gas
#opt(method=lbfgs)

XYZ structure.xyz

Model Options

Option Default Description
dtype float32 The current exports accept FP32 inputs only; native internal precision is retained.

Limitations

  • Electronic state: all five NequIP and Allegro-FM models require charge 0 and multiplicity 1.
  • Chemical coverage: the PeptideCS2 NequIP models cover peptides containing H, C, N, O and S. Allegro-FM covers 89 elements (Z = 1–83 and 89–94).
  • Atomic charges and dipoles: neither property is provided by the NequIP or Allegro-FM calculator.
  • Reference environment: the PeptideCS2 NequIP models retain their gas-phase or COSMO-water training reference. The COSMO contribution is already part of the COSMO-water models and their energy derivatives.
  • Additional implicit solvent: MAPLE's experimental GB-polar/QEq correction supports non-periodic single-point energies only (#sp with experimental=true); solvent forces, Hessians and HVPs are unavailable.
