MACE

MACE is an equivariant message-passing architecture that uses higher-order atomic cluster expansion features to describe many-body interactions. MAPLE provides MACE-OFF, MACE-LES, EGRET, MACE-OMol and MACE-Polar models within this family.

MACE-OFF and MACE-LES

MACE-OFF23 and MACE-OFF24 use ωB97M-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVPPD energy and force references. OFF23 uses SPICE v1 with additional molecular conformations and water clusters; OFF24 includes the expanded SPICE v2 data. MACE-LES-OFF uses the OFF23 training/validation split and adds latent Ewald summation for long-range electrostatics; its latent charges are internal model variables. The OFF and LES models listed below cover H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Br and I.

Model Keyword Parameters Best For
MACE-OFF23 (Small) mace-off-23s ~0.69M Fast screening, large systems, speed-critical workflows
MACE-OFF23 (Medium) mace-off-23m ~1.43M Routine organic-molecule and condensed-phase calculations
MACE-OFF23 (Large) mace-off-23l ~4.71M Accuracy-focused organic-molecule calculations
MACE-OFF23 (B-Medium) mace-off-23bm ~1.43M Organic molecules and molecular clusters with a 6 Å local cutoff
MACE-OFF24 (Medium) mace-off-24m ~1.43M Organic molecules and clusters with expanded SPICE v2 coverage
MACE-LES-OFF mace-les-off ~1.89M Organic systems with long-range electrostatic interactions

Important

mace-off-23l requires significantly more GPU memory than the smaller variants. If you encounter out-of-memory errors, switch to mace-off-23m or mace-off-23s, or use #device=cpu.

MACE-OMol

mace-omol-0 uses the OMol25 molecular reference at ωB97M-V/def2-TZVPD. It covers H through Bi (Z = 1–83), accepts charge and multiplicity from the coordinate block, and supports periodic cells.

#model=mace-omol-0
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs)

0 1
Li   0.000000    0.000000    0.000000
F    1.570000    0.000000    0.000000

EGRET

EGRET models use the MACE architecture with ωB97M-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVPPD reference data. EGRET-1 uses the MACE-OFF23 training set; EGRET-1E adds VectorQM24 main-group structures, and EGRET-1T adds Transition1x and Coley3+2 reaction data. EGRET-1M and EGRET-1S are smaller versions of EGRET-1. EGRET-1, EGRET-1M, EGRET-1S and EGRET-1T cover H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Br and I; EGRET-1E also covers Si.

Keyword Variant Cutoff (Å)
egret-1 MACE-OFF23 training data 6.0
egret-1e Additional VectorQM24 data (+Si) 6.0
egret-1m Medium 5.0
egret-1s Small 4.5
egret-1t Additional reaction and transition-state data 6.0 
#model=egret-1s
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs)

XYZ /path/to/drug_molecule.xyz

MACE-Polar

The mace-polar-1s, mace-polar-1m and mace-polar-1l models add explicit electrostatic response to the MACE family. Their OMol25 energy and force reference is ωB97M-V/def2-TZVPD. These models cover H through Bi (Z = 1–83), accept charge and multiplicity, and provide physical atomic charges and dipoles.

#model=mace-polar-1s
#sp

XYZ structure.xyz

Calculator Capabilities

  • Hessian and HVP: all MACE-OFF, MACE-LES, EGRET, MACE-OMol and MACE-Polar models provide analytic Hessians by default, numerical Hessians, and Hessian–vector products.
  • Periodic systems: all five model series support periodic cells, with stress and virial output.
  • Charge and multiplicity: MACE-OFF, MACE-LES and EGRET require charge 0 and multiplicity 1. MACE-OMol and MACE-Polar accept the charge and multiplicity specified in the coordinate block.
  • Atomic charges and dipoles: MACE-Polar provides both properties. MACE-LES latent charges are internal model variables.
  • Implicit solvent: the experimental GB-polar/QEq correction supports non-periodic single-point energies only (#sp with experimental=true); solvent forces, Hessians and HVPs are unavailable.

Usage Examples

Quick screening with MACE-OFF23 Small

#model=mace-off-23s
#device=gpu0
#opt(level=loose)

XYZ /path/to/molecule.xyz

Optimization with MACE-OFF23 Large

#model=mace-off-23l
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs, level=tight)

XYZ /path/to/optimized_guess.xyz

Frequency calculation

#model=mace-off-23m
#device=gpu0
#freq

XYZ /path/to/optimized_geometry.xyz

CPU fallback for large systems

#model=mace-off-23s
#device=cpu
#opt(level=loose)

XYZ /path/to/large_system.xyz

Limitations

  • Electronic state: MACE-OFF, MACE-LES and EGRET require charge 0 and multiplicity 1.
  • Elements: MACE-OFF, MACE-LES and EGRET cover H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Br and I; EGRET-1E also supports Si. MACE-OMol and MACE-Polar cover H–Bi (Z = 1–83).
  • No D4 dispersion. MAPLE's separate D4 correction is not supported by these model series.
  • Implicit solvent: the experimental correction is limited to non-periodic single-point energies.
