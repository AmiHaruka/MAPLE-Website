SO3LR

SO3LR is a pretrained force-field family for molecular and biomolecular simulations. It couples SO3krates to explicit repulsion, electrostatics and dispersion, with environment-dependent charges and dispersion parameters describing noncovalent interactions.

Pretrained Model Series

MAPLE provides the original SO3LR model and three SO3LR Tauri models from release 2606.

SO3LR Tauri aims to describe proteins, nucleic acids, lipids and carbohydrates with a common potential. Its scope extends to ionic environments, metal-containing systems and reactive chemistry, without fitting a separate force field for each system.

The original model uses ZBL-inspired repulsion and a single Hirshfeld volume ratio per atom for dispersion. Tauri uses fixed, pair-specific Nordlund–Lehtola–Hobler (NLH) repulsion and separately learned polarizability and C6 ratios for quantum Drude oscillator (QDO) dispersion.

Keyword Model Elements Cutoff (Å)
so3lr Original SO3LR H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl 4.5
so3lr-tauri-s Tauri small, 2606 H–At (Z = 1–85) 4.5
so3lr-tauri-m Tauri medium, 2606 H–At (Z = 1–85) 4.5
so3lr-tauri-l Tauri large, 2606 H–At (Z = 1–85) 5.0

Training Reference and Chemical Space

The original SO3LR uses PBE0+MBD references for about four million neutral and charged molecular structures. Tauri combines roughly 148 million structures across 85 elements, including ions, metal-containing systems, polymers and biomolecular fragments.

The supplied Tauri models use the production head, fine-tuned with PBE0+MBD(-NL) energies and forces together with ωB97M-V forces from OMol25 and OPoly26.

Charge and Multiplicity

The models accept total charge and spin multiplicity through the coordinate block. Atomic charges and molecular dipoles are available.

Calculator Capabilities

  • Hessian and HVP: the original SO3LR and SO3LR Tauri models provide analytic Hessians by default, numerical Hessians, and Hessian–vector products.
  • Charge and multiplicity: both model generations accept the total charge and spin multiplicity specified in the coordinate block.
  • Atomic charges and dipoles: both model generations provide atomic partial charges and molecular dipoles.
  • Periodic systems: SO3LR and SO3LR Tauri support periodic cells, with stress and virial output.

Usage

#model=so3lr-tauri-m
#opt(method=lbfgs)

XYZ structure.xyz

Model Options

Option Default Description
lr auto auto, cutoff, ewald or pme. Auto uses the molecular route for isolated systems and PME for full three-dimensional periodic cells.
dtype float32 Supported value: float32.

Limitations

  • Chemical coverage: the original SO3LR was trained on H, C, N, O, F, P, S and Cl. SO3LR Tauri extends the training coverage to H–At (Z = 1–85).
  • Boundary conditions: periodic calculations require full three-dimensional periodicity. The ewald and pme options cannot be used for isolated molecules.
  • Additional implicit solvent: MAPLE's experimental GB-polar/QEq correction supports non-periodic single-point energies only (#sp with experimental=true); solvent forces, Hessians and HVPs are unavailable.
