ANI Models

ANI uses radial and angular atomic environment vectors (AEVs) as inputs to atomic neural networks. MAPLE provides the pretrained variants below for neutral singlet systems.

Available Variants

Model Keyword Elements Training Level Best For
ANI-2x ani2x H, C, N, O, F, S, Cl ωB97X/6-31G(d) General organic chemistry
ANI-1x ani1x H, C, N, O ωB97X/6-31G(d) HCNO molecular systems
ANI-1ccx ani1ccx H, C, N, O CCSD(T)*/CBS transfer learning Small-molecule energetics
ANI-1xnr ani1xnr H, C, N, O BLYP/TZV2P Reactive molecular and condensed-phase chemistry
ANI-2xr ani2xr H, C, N, O, F, S, Cl ωB97X/6-31G(d) Molecular dynamics and geometry optimization of organic molecules
ANI-2dr ani2dr H, C, N, O, F, S, Cl B97-3c Organic molecules and clusters with native dispersion
SnnANI-2xr snn-ani2xr H, C, N, O, F, S, Cl ωB97X/6-31G(d) Neutral organic-molecule energies and forces
ANI-MBIS ani-mbis H, C, N, O, F, S, Cl Energies: ωB97X/6-31G(d) Molecular energies, atomic charges and dipoles

DFT-D4 Dispersion Correction

MAPLE provides an optional DFT-D4 correction for non-periodic ANI calculations. It adds a dispersion energy to the selected ANI model. Periodic inputs and ani2dr reject this extra correction.

#model=ani2x
#d4
#opt(method=lbfgs)

Tip

ani2dr includes short-range repulsion and D3 dispersion in its model energy. Use it without #d4.

Calculator Capabilities

  • Hessian: analytic Hessians are used by default; numerical Hessians are also available.
  • HVP: ANI provides Hessian–vector products for HVP-enabled workflows.
  • Periodic systems: ANI supports periodic cells, with stress and virial output.
  • Atomic charges and dipoles: ani-mbis provides both properties. All eight ANI models require charge 0 and multiplicity 1.
  • Implicit solvent: the experimental GB-polar/QEq correction supports non-periodic single-point energies only (#sp with experimental=true); solvent forces, Hessians and HVPs are unavailable.

Usage Examples

General organic optimization

#model=ani2x
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs)

0 1
C    0.000000    0.000000    0.000000
O    1.200000    0.000000    0.000000
H   -0.540000    0.940000    0.000000
H   -0.540000   -0.940000    0.000000

High-accuracy with coupled-cluster potential

#model=ani1ccx
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs, level=extratight)

0 1
O    0.000000    0.000000    0.117300
H    0.000000    0.756900   -0.469200
H    0.000000   -0.756900   -0.469200

With D4 dispersion for non-covalent interactions

#model=ani2x
#d4
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs, level=tight)

XYZ /path/to/dimer_complex.xyz

Limitations

  • No charge/multiplicity support. ANI models assume neutral, singlet ground state; use AIMNet2-NSE or UMA omol for charged/open-shell inputs.
  • Elements: the 1x family covers H, C, N, O; the 2x family and ANI-MBIS cover the seven elements listed above.
  • Atomic charges: charge and dipole output is available for ani-mbis; ordinary ANI models provide energies and forces without that charge model.
  • Implicit solvation is energy-only. MAPLE's experimental GB-polar/QEq correction can be requested for #sp with experimental=true; solvent forces, optimization, MD, TS, scan, and frequency workflows remain unsupported for that correction.
