FeNNol Models

FeNNol is a JAX-based framework for building force-field-enhanced neural network potentials. MAPLE includes the FeNNix-Bio1 model series built with this framework.

FeNNix-Bio1 Family

FeNNix-Bio1 targets molecular and condensed-phase biomolecular systems. Its training data extend SPICE2 with additional molecular conformations, solvated ions and dissociation configurations. The quantum-chemical reference uses ωB97M-D3(BJ) with ccECP effective core potentials and their aug-cc-pVTZ basis sets.

Both models use v1.0-finetuneIons, which additionally fine-tunes solvated Na, K and Cl ions against AMOEBA03 data.

Model Keyword Best For
FeNNix-Bio1S fennix-bio-1s High-throughput molecular and biomolecular simulations
FeNNix-Bio1M fennix-bio-1m Biomolecular and molecular-cluster simulations

Important

The medium variant (fennix-bio-1m) requires more computational resources than the small variant (fennix-bio-1s).

Element input range: the current MAPLE exports encode H–Rn (Z = 1–86). The training and application scope is molecular and biomolecular chemistry, including solvated ions.

Model Options

Option Default Description
dtype float32 Supported value: float32.

Charge and Multiplicity

Both ion-finetuned models accept total molecular charge and require multiplicity 1. Internal charge-hypothesis channels remain model features and are not exposed as physical atomic charges or dipoles.

#model=fennix-bio-1s
#opt(method=lbfgs)

0 1
C    0.000000    0.000000    0.000000
O    1.230000    0.000000    0.000000
H   -0.540000    0.940000    0.000000
H   -0.540000   -0.940000    0.000000

Calculator Capabilities

  • Units: MAPLE reports energies in Hartree and forces in Hartree per Angstrom.
  • Hessian: analytic Hessians are used by default; numerical Hessians are also available.
  • HVP: FeNNix-Bio1S and FeNNix-Bio1M provide Hessian–vector products.
  • Periodic systems: both models support full three-dimensional periodic cells, with stress and virial output.
  • Solvent / D4: the experimental GB-polar/QEq correction supports non-periodic single-point energies only (#sp with experimental=true); solvent forces, Hessians and HVPs are unavailable. D4 is not supported.

Usage Examples

Geometry optimization with FeNNix-Bio1S

#model=fennix-bio-1s
#device=gpu0
#opt(level=loose)

XYZ /path/to/structure.xyz

Tight geometry optimization with FeNNix-Bio1M

#model=fennix-bio-1m
#device=gpu0
#opt(method=lbfgs, level=tight)

XYZ /path/to/structure.xyz

Limitations

  • Spin: the current Bio1 models require multiplicity 1.
  • Atomic charges and dipoles: these outputs are not available for FeNNix-Bio1 in MAPLE.
  • Model scope: the built-in models use the ion-finetuned Bio1 release for molecular and biomolecular systems.
