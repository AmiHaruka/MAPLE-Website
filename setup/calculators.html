Calculator Backends & Capability Matrix

This page summarizes the calculator layer documented for the MAPLE 0.1.4 development branch. The entries describe the current development implementation. Use it to compare the available models and calculation options.

Development note

Backend capability gates are enforced before or during calculator construction: electronic-state inputs, periodic cells, Hessian modes and model options are checked against the selected calculator and model.

Selection Rules

  • Periodic systems: AIMNet2, FeNNix and SO3LR require full three-dimensional periodicity. Periodic UMA inputs require an explicit FAIR-Chem task; MAPLE does not yet provide UMA stress or virial output.
  • Electronic state: ANI, GRACE-OFF, NequIP/Allegro-FM, MACE-OFF, MACE-LES and EGRET require charge 0 and multiplicity 1. Charged and open-shell inputs require the charge/spin conditioning described in the selected model's card; FeNNix requires multiplicity 1.
  • D4 dispersion: the optional D4 correction is available for non-periodic ANI inputs, except ani2dr. Other model families retain their native treatment.
  • HVP-enabled Dimer: ANI, AIMNet2, FeNNol, MACE, GRACE, SO3LR and NequIP/Allegro-FM provide native Hessian–vector products. UMA uses the common force-difference route.
  • Additional implicit solvent: the experimental GB-polar/QEq correction supports non-periodic single-point energies only. Solvent forces, Hessians and HVPs are unavailable.
